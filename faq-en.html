Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our rental service.

What documents do I need to rent?

We require the following documents:

  • Valid Driving License
  • ID Card or Passport
  • Credit Card (under the driver's name)
Is my driving license valid in your country?

All European (EU) licenses are valid in Italy.

Are my credit card details protected?

Yes, your credit card details are used exclusively for rental security purposes.

Am I insured in case of an accident?

The vehicle itself is covered by Third Party Liability insurance (RC), which is mandatory by Italian law. This covers damages to third parties but does not cover personal injury to the driver.

What is included in the rental?

The rental includes third-party insurance (RC), two helmets, two hygienic under-helmet liners, and a top case.

With what fuel level should I return the vehicle?

The vehicle must be returned with a full tank of fuel; otherwise, a refueling fee will be charged.

Is there a mileage limit?

Go wherever you want… within the local area! Unlimited mileage included, ask for details.

Is the rental day 24 hours?

No, a standard rental day is from 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM unless further agreements are made.

Can I drive on the highway (Autostrada)?

Since December 14, 2024, 125cc scooters (with 120cc thermal engines or 6kW electric motors) can circulate on motorways and ring roads in Italy. You must be of legal age and hold a valid driving license (category A, B, or A1/A2) for at least two years. This regulation updates the Italian highway code, bringing Italy in line with European standards.

What to do in case of an accident?

Contact us immediately at the number provided with the vehicle documents and fill out the Accident Report Form (CAI/CID) found in the document pouch.

Who is responsible for traffic fines?

Any traffic fines received during the rental period will be redirected to the renter responsible for the violation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our rental service.

How much does a Vespa 125 cost?

Vespa 125 is €70 per day. Includes third-party insurance, two helmets, top case and lock. It's our premium option for the Cinque Terre – book in advance on WhatsApp as availability is limited.

How much does a SYM 125 scooter cost?

Our SYM Symphony 125 (or similar 125cc) starts at €50 per day. Same inclusions as the Vespa, perfect value for exploring La Spezia and the coast.

Where can I park the scooter in Cinque Terre?

Each village has dedicated paid motorcycle parking: Riomaggiore (upper parking), Manarola (above town), Corniglia (at the entrance), Vernazza (before the downhill), Monterosso (Fegina seafront). Scooters avoid ZTL restrictions and park much easier than cars.

Can I visit all five villages in one day by scooter?

Yes – it's the best way. From La Spezia take the SP370: Riomaggiore (15 min), Manarola (5), Corniglia (10), Vernazza (15), Monterosso (10). With photo stops you can complete the loop in 6-8 hours and return by 8pm.

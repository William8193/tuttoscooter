Visiting Cinque Terre by Scooter

Visiting the Cinque Terre by scooter is an unforgettable experience. The coastal road connecting La Spezia to Riomaggiore, Manarola, Corniglia, Vernazza, and Monterosso offers unique views perched above the sea.


While trains are often overcrowded and hot in summer, the scooter gives you the sea breeze and the ability to stop at every scenic viewpoint for a souvenir photo. It's the best alternative for those who want to experience authentic Liguria, away from the crowds.

Scooter vs Train: Which is better for Cinque Terre?

Many tourists choose the train, but the scooter offers unparalleled advantages for those who want to truly experience the territory.

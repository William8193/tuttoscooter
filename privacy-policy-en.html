This policy describes how Tuttoscooter di Moncini Stefano collects, uses, and protects users' personal data in accordance with Article 13 of EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Data Controller

The Data Controller is Tuttoscooter di Moncini Stefano, with registered office at Via Michele Rossi, 67 – 19126 La Spezia (SP), VAT No. 01172420117. For communications regarding data protection, please use the phone numbers or email address provided in the Contact section of the website.

2. Types of Data Collected and Methods

Our website is primarily informational in nature. We collect data in the following ways:

  • Voluntary Contact: When the user uses the WhatsApp buttons, makes phone calls, or sends emails to request information or quotes.
  • Browsing Data: Technologies such as localStorage are used exclusively for technical purposes, such as storing the user's preferred language (preferredLang).

3. Purpose of Processing

Data is processed exclusively to:

  • Provide quotes and information on the availability of cars and scooters for hire.
  • Manage booking procedures and the execution of rental agreements.
  • Ensure proper use of the website (e.g. displaying content in the correct language).

4. Secure Handling of Credit Card Data

For car and scooter bookings, credit card details are required solely as a security guarantee. This website has no online payment system and does not store any financial data. Such information is provided by the user exclusively through direct and secure channels (phone or encrypted messaging) and is handled via protected POS terminals at our operational premises.

5. Legal Basis

Processing is based on the consent of the data subject (expressed by submitting a contact request) and on the performance of pre-contractual or contractual measures related to the rental service.

6. Retention Period

Contact data is retained for the time strictly necessary to respond to requests. Should a rental agreement be concluded, data will be retained for the period required by applicable tax and civil law obligations.

7. Rights of the Data Subject

Under the GDPR, users have the right to:

  • Access their personal data and request a copy.
  • Request rectification or erasure of data (right to be forgotten).
  • Object to processing or request a restriction thereof.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali).

To exercise these rights, please contact the Data Controller using the details available on the website.

In case of any discrepancy between the English and the Italian version of this policy, the Italian version shall prevail.

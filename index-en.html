Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent a Vespa in La Spezia for Cinque Terre?

Vespa 125 is available from €70 per day. Price varies by season and availability. Helmets and liability insurance included. Ride with European B license. Pickup at cruise terminal exit or Via Michele Rossi 67.

How much does it cost to rent a 125cc Sym scooter?

Sym 125 scooter is available from €50 per day, with variable rates in high demand periods. Helmets and insurance included. Same B license. Same pickup points.

Where can I park the scooter in Cinque Terre?

There are designated parking areas at the entrance of each village. We will provide you with a map with safe and recommended parking points at rental.

Can I visit all Cinque Terre villages with the scooter?

Yes! The 125cc scooter is perfect for visiting Riomaggiore, Manarola, Corniglia, Vernazza and Monterosso. The scenic road offers spectacular sea views.
